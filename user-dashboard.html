Your plant care guide

Simple steps to help your plants flourish.

01

Water wisely

Feel the soil before watering. Water thoroughly when needed, and make sure excess water can drain away.

02

Understand sunlight

Check each plant's light requirements. Some plants prefer indirect light while others need direct sun.

03

Give roots room

Choose containers with drainage holes and suitable potting mix. Repot when roots outgrow their container.

04

Keep leaves healthy

Remove dead leaves and gently clean dusty foliage. Check leaves regularly for signs of pests.

05

Feed at the right time

Use a suitable fertiliser when needed. Follow product instructions and avoid applying more than recommended.

06

Observe and learn

Notice changes in leaf colour, growth and soil moisture. Adjust care according to the plant and season.
